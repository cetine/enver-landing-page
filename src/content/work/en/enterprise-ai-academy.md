## Challenge

The leadership team had access to capable AI tools but no framework for integrating generative AI into the product roadmap. The gap stalled innovation, as decision-makers struggled to identify use cases beyond simple efficiency gains.

## Approach

Designed a "GenAI in Product" executive program that coaches CXOs on roadmap development through a series of workshops, connecting technical capability to business strategy.

## Impact

More than forty leaders completed the program, over twenty high-value use cases moved into launch, and it became a blueprint for enterprise-wide adoption.
