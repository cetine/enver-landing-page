## Challenge

Route planning depended on dispatcher experience and static schedules, leaving fleet capacity underused. Driver availability, traffic, and delivery time-windows sat in disconnected systems, which caused frequent missed SLAs and excessive empty-mile runs that inflated fuel costs.

## Approach

Built a predictive dispatch platform that combines real-time telematics, weather, and traffic data with an optimization core. Loads are assigned by predicted ETA, hours-of-service compliance, and vehicle capacity, and routes rebalance as conditions change.

## Impact

On-time delivery climbed into the mid-nineties, empty miles dropped by roughly 30 percent, and the network now saves over one and a half million euros a year.
