## Challenge

Cross-referencing building information models against evolving local safety codes was manual and error-prone. Engineers spent weeks on visual inspection, which created a bottleneck in the design phase and raised the risk of costly retrofits during construction.

## Approach

Built a geometry-aware engine that parses Industry Foundation Classes (IFC) files and validates building models against local safety codes automatically. Checks that took weeks now run in minutes.

## Impact

Review cycles run around 60 % faster, the majority of compliance checks are automated, and no compliance misses have surfaced.
